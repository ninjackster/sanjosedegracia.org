Our Town

Where the highlands
slow everything down

Nestled at 1,980 meters above sea level, San José de Gracia is a historic town of cobblestone streets, a centuries-old church, and the kind of unhurried warmth that Jalisco's highlands are known for. Come for a weekend, stay for the mezcal and the sunsets.

What to Do

Eat, explore,
and unwind

From mezcal country to mountain trails — there's more to discover here than most visitors expect.

🥘

Food & Restaurants

Taste the flavors of Jalisco at local fondas and family kitchens. Birria, pozole, and slow-cooked dishes that have been perfected over generations.

🌄

Points of Interest

Visit the historic Parroquia de San José, hike Cerro Chico and Cerro Gordo, and take in panoramic views of the Altos highlands.

🌙

Nightlife & Mezcal

The region sits in the heart of agave country. End your evenings with artisanal mezcal and the easy company of locals.

🛍️

Shops & Markets

Pick up handcrafted goods, local produce, and regional specialties. The plaza comes alive on weekends with vendors and families.
